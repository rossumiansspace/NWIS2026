02

Process flow — the SDLC

Every project we take on follows the Software Development Life Cycle, in seven stages:

  • 01
    PlanningDefine the project's goals, scope, and feasibility.
  • 02
    Requirement analysisGather and document exactly what the client needs, usually at the Meeting Area.
  • 03
    DesignPlan the system architecture and UI/UX before any code is written.
  • 04
    DevelopmentThe student engineering team builds the system.
  • 05
    TestingThe QA team checks the system for bugs and gaps before release.
  • 06
    DeploymentThe finished system is launched and handed over to the client.
  • 07
    MaintenanceThe team monitors and supports the system after launch.
