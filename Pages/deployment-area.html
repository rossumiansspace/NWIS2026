Deployment process

The final two stages of our SDLC — Deployment and Maintenance — broken down:

  • 01
    StagingFinal build is deployed to a staging environment for one last check.
  • 02
    QA sign-offTesting team confirms the staging build is stable.
  • 03
    Production (Deployment stage)The system is deployed to its live server or hosting — this closes out SDLC stage 6.
  • 04
    Monitoring (Maintenance stage)The team watches for issues and provides support after launch — SDLC stage 7.
