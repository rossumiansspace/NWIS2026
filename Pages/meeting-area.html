Meeting Area

Where requirements get clarified in detail before a project moves into design and development.

01

Project requirements

What we cover during a consultation, straight from our intake form:

  • The business problem — what isn't working today
  • The current process — how it's handled now, manually or otherwise
  • The desired system — what the client wants built
  • Required features — logins, database, reports, search, dashboard, and more
  • Target users — who will actually use the system
02

Sample forms

Real documents used throughout a project's lifecycle at Lagro Tech Hub:

  • 01
    Client Requirement & Interview FormAnnex A · Form A-1 — captures the business problem, current process, desired system, required features, and target users.
  • 02
    Commercial Cost Quotation FormAnnex A · Form A-2 — itemizes services (design, development, database, testing, deployment, training) and cost.
  • 03
    Client Negotiation & Scope RecordAnnex A · Form A-3 — records package options presented, the client's request, and the final agreed terms.
03

Consultation guide

What a typical session looks like:

  • 01
    InterviewThe team fills out the Client Requirement & Interview Form together with the client.
  • 02
    QuotationA Commercial Cost Quotation is prepared based on the services needed.
  • 03
    NegotiationPackage options are discussed and recorded on the Client Negotiation & Scope Record.
  • 04
    Sign-offOnce terms are agreed, the client proceeds to the Client Desk for the formal agreement.
