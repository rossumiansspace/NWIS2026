Welcome message

Welcome to Lagro Tech Hub, the student-managed IT Solutions Center.

Every project you'll see today started the same way: a client request. What follows is the full process our student teams go through to turn that request into a working solution, planning, design, development, testing, and deployment.

This space is organized as a walkthrough of that process. Scan the QR code at each station to explore what happens there, and follow the numbered path for the complete client journey. Our student team is on hand throughout to answer any questions.

Thank you for visiting, we hope you enjoy seeing what we build.
